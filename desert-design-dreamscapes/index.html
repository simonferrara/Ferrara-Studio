Good to know

Straight
answers.

Is the design consultation really free?

Yes. We come to your home, walk the property and talk through your ideas, style and budget — no cost and no pressure. You leave with a clear picture and proposed next steps.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. Desert Design Dreamscapes LLC holds Arizona ROC license #368643 (CR-21 Hardscaping & Irrigation) and is licensed, bonded and insured.

What warranty do you offer?

A 2-year warranty on workmanship and a 3-month warranty on plants.

How does payment work?

Projects are paid in three installments. Card payments carry a 3.8% processing fee. Cancellations are subject to a $500 fee.

Do you handle HOA approval?

HOA approvals are submitted by the homeowner. Your design package — renders, plans and material specs — gives you everything you need for the submission.

Can I just buy the design?

Absolutely. Our Full Landscape Design package includes concepts, photorealistic 3D renders, construction-ready blueprints and a complete materials list.
