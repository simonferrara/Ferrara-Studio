What it does, and what it never does

  • Answers every chat, at any hour

    Nights, weekends and the minutes when everyone in the office is on the phone.

  • Speaks English and Spanish

    It switches the moment a customer writes in Spanish and stays there. Your summary always arrives in English.

  • Flags emergencies

    No cooling in the heat or no heat in a freeze gets your on-call number straight away, with the details already taken.

  • Puts safety first

    A gas smell or a carbon monoxide alarm gets one fixed message before anything else: leave the house, call 911 and the gas company.

  • Never quotes a price

    A technician confirms the price before any work begins. The assistant turns “how much?” into a requested visit instead.

  • Only says what your site says

    Hours, service area, services, license, financing. Anything else, it notes for your team and says someone will confirm.
