What we fix, tune and install

Residential heating and cooling, all makes and models. Every visit starts with a proper diagnosis.

ServiceWhat we doWhat you get

  • Air conditioning

    Diagnostics, repair, maintenance and full system replacement. Technicians check airflow, refrigerant, electrical components and overall operation before recommending anything.

    The cause found first, so the cooling stays fixed.

    Book air conditioning service

  • AC check-up

    A full inspection: thermostat, coils, refrigerant pressures, capacitors, drain line, amp draw and safety controls, with written findings and recommendations.

    Small problems caught before they become a summer breakdown.

    Book ac check-up service

  • Furnace

    System evaluation, repair, seasonal service and new furnace installation, using manufacturer-approved parts and procedures.

    Steady, safe heat when the cold front arrives.

    Book furnace service

  • Heat pump

    Diagnostics, repair, maintenance and installation for systems that heat and cool from a single unit.

    One system kept efficient through both seasons.

    Book heat pump service

  • Ductless mini-splits

    Installation, repair and maintenance for individual rooms, additions and homes without traditional ductwork.

    Room-by-room temperature control, no ducts required.

    Book ductless mini-splits service

  • Ductwork

    Inspection, repair, sealing and replacement of residential duct systems.

    Balanced airflow and even temperatures in every room.

    Book ductwork service

  • Indoor air quality

    Air filtration, purification and humidity control, starting with an evaluation of your airflow and ventilation.

    Cleaner air and steadier humidity inside the house.

    Book indoor air quality service

Commercial work, too. Pre-lease HVAC inspections, rooftop units (RTUs), split systems, make-up air units, exhaust and supply air systems, ductless mini-splits, modular building HVAC units, equipment change-outs and tenant improvements.

Paying for it. Flexible financing is available for new installations and major repairs, and Brown’s Service Agreement covers preventive maintenance ahead of each season. Ask when you call.
