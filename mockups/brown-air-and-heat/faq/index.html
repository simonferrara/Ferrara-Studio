Why is my air conditioner blowing warm air?

Warm air can be caused by thermostat settings, a clogged air filter, low refrigerant, electrical problems or compressor failure. A professional inspection identifies the exact cause and restores cooling.

How often should my air conditioner be serviced?

At least once a year, ideally before summer. Regular maintenance improves efficiency, prevents breakdowns and extends the life of the system. Heating systems should be serviced once a year before winter.

Do you offer 24/7 emergency HVAC service?

Yes. If your system stops working during extreme heat or cold, call 281-998-2665 at any hour. On-call service is subject to technician availability, repairs are limited to truck stock, and a service fee and after-hours fee apply.

Why is my air conditioner freezing up?

Freezing is usually caused by restricted airflow, dirty filters or low refrigerant levels. It can damage the system if it is not addressed promptly.

Why is my heater blowing cold air?

Thermostat settings, ignition issues, dirty filters or duct problems are the usual causes. A technician can diagnose and resolve the issue quickly.

When should I replace my system instead of repairing it?

If the system is more than 10 to 15 years old, needs frequent repairs or runs inefficiently, replacement may be more cost-effective. Most AC systems last 10 to 15 years with proper maintenance.
