Plumbing services

Hidden leak or damaged pipe? We find it and fix it.

Water leaks rarely announce themselves. In North Texas, expansive clay soil and hard water put constant stress on the pipes in your home, so small problems become big ones fast.

Leak detectionPipe repairWater heaters, drains & more

Leak detection

Our licensed plumbers use electronic leak detection to find the problem before opening anything up, which minimizes access work and gets the repair started sooner.

Signs you may have a leak

  • Water stains or damp spots on walls, ceilings or floors
  • Unexplained increases in your monthly water bill
  • A sudden drop in water pressure throughout the house
  • Warm spots on the floor or the sound of running water with everything off
  • Musty odors near walls, under cabinets or at floor level
