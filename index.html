[ 10 — FAQ ]

Website
FAQ.

What does “human + AI” actually mean?

Simon handles strategy, taste and direction on every project. AI handles production — code, images, video and motion — so you get studio-level work in days instead of weeks, reviewed by a person at every step.

Can I use the AI video commercially?

Yes. You get the final files for your brand’s ads, social and website. We only use tools whose terms allow commercial use, and we never imitate real people or other brands.

Do I really get my website in 7 days?

Yes — 7 business days from the moment we receive your brief. If we're ever late for a reason on our end, we refund $100 per day of delay.

Do I need tech knowledge?

Zero. You fill a 10-question brief about your business and we handle everything — design, copy, hosting, domain connection and setup. You just approve the result.

What if I don't like how it looks?

You don't pay a single dollar until you approve the design. Business and Premium include unlimited revisions; Starter includes 2 rounds. If you're genuinely not happy after revisions, we refund 100%.

Who writes the content?

We do. You give us the key details in the brief and we handle all copywriting, headlines and calls to action. You can also send your own copy if you prefer.

Will my site show up on Google?

Every site includes on-page SEO — meta titles, descriptions, heading structure, image alt text and schema markup. Business and Premium include Google Analytics and Search Console setup.

How does the $99/month plan work?

$0 down. You approve the design first, then pay $99/month with a 12-month minimum — hosting, domain, monthly content changes and support included. After 12 months it continues month to month and you can cancel anytime. The domain is registered in your name, and if you ever leave you can buy the site files for a one-time fee.

What is the $299/month Website + AI Front Desk plan?

Everything in the $99/month Website Plan, plus an AI front desk that answers your website chat and phone calls 24/7, books appointments, captures leads and emails you a summary. Same terms: $0 down, 12-month minimum, then month to month. It's in early access with limited spots, so we set it up together with you.

What happens after the site is live?

With a one-time package you own the site completely. We hand you all credentials and a video walkthrough, and if you need changes later we'll quote them. On the $99/month plan, hosting, updates and support are already included.
